Madeline "Maddie" Rubin is a writer and a dabbler from New York City. She is currently a Neubauer Fellow and a PhD student in Cinema and Media Studies at the University of Chicago. She received undergraduate degrees in English and Risk, Data, and Financial Engineering from Duke University.

Her work and research spans documentary, comedy, and coding, and she is interested in how extreme human commitment survives with and against modern technology. Her various projects have been featured in The New York Times, the Los Angeles Review of Books, and The Believer.
